Reitz_hmmdetails.txt = hmmdetails.txt file for metallophore biosynthesis profiles from Reitz et al. 2026, eLife (can be downloaded from supplementary materials)
Crits-Christoph_hmmdetails.txt = hmmdetails.txt file for transporter domains associated with metallophore export/import. This file is also from Reitz et al 2026, but refers to the findings of Crits-Christoph et al. 2021, Genome Research

The profile HMMs used for searching were gathered from supplementary materials provided in Reitz et al. 2026, eLife and searched for in predicted proteomes using PyHMMER. The files Reitz_etal_2026.hmm and Crits-Christoph_etal_2021.hmm referenced in the PyHMMER scripts are simply concatenations of the individual profile HMMs.

All_Proteins.faa is simply a concatenation of all proteins from the Bofasa_Prep_Results/Genome_Processing/Proteomes/ folder (which can be found on the associated Zenodo repository)
